How to play

Saber

Swipe fast across zombies once they step into the glowing slash zone.

Guns

Click or tap to shoot. You start with the Pop Blaster. Five more guns come in airdrops.

Beat + combo

Hit on the drum beat for 1.5× points. Every 5 kills in a row raises your multiplier, up to ×8.

1–7 or wheel to switch weapons · Q last weapon · T tap the beat · P pause · M mute
