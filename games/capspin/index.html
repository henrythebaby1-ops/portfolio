How Competition Works

  • Accuracy Round: Spin toward a marked target zone with tiered scoring rings.
  • Speed Round: Fastest clean cap removal wins. One official attempt only.
  • Style Round: Judges score creativity, difficulty, smoothness, and control.

Official Sessions

Structured time windows keep scheduling and judging consistent.

Championship Path

  • Compete at your locked campus
  • Climb campus rankings
  • Win Campus Champion bracket
  • Top 3 per campus advance to Inter-Campus Finals
  • Compete for the Golden Cap Trophy

Fair Play Standard

Official league play uses only approved Uncapped Marker Caps. Modified, weighted, damaged, or personal caps are prohibited to protect equal conditions for all competitors.

Each season runs for 4 weeks, then rankings reset. Returning champions receive leaderboard badges.
