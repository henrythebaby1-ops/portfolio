CapSpin Championship Rules

League authority is held by Uncapped. Only the designated owner account may issue administrative overrides, disciplinary rulings, and final standings decisions.

1. Divisions

  • Solo Division: individual ranking and titles.
  • Team Division: 2-3 students, official team name, and designated captain required.

2. Official Times

  • 11:20 AM (Lunch)
  • 3:00 PM (After School)

3. Match Rounds

  • Accuracy Round: target zone tier scoring.
  • Speed Round: one clean attempt only.
  • Style Round: judged creativity and control.

4. Advancement

  • Top 8 per campus enter elimination bracket.
  • Winner becomes Campus Champion.
  • Top 3 per campus advance to Inter-Campus Finals.

5. Equipment Rules

  • Only official Uncapped Marker Caps are allowed.
  • Provided or verified caps only.
  • Modified, weighted, or damaged caps are prohibited.

6. Campus Lock Rule

  • Campus selection locks for the full season.
  • Cross-campus competition is not permitted.
  • Transfers require League Owner override.

7. Safety and Conduct

  • Spin only inside designated competition zones.
  • Throwing caps at people is prohibited.
  • Penalties: warning, deduction, or disqualification.

8. Season & Resets

  • Season length: 4 weeks.
  • Registration is free and instant (no approval).
  • Rankings reset after each season.
  • Returning champions earn leaderboard badges.

9. Administrative Powers (Uncapped Only)

The owner dashboard can view all registrations, manage approvals, lock sign-ups, adjust schedules, override brackets, remove participants, issue rulings, and finalize standings.

Campus League: Sunnyvale, Strawberry Park, Campbell.
