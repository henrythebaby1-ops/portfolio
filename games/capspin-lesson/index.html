Capspin Lesson Place

This is not connected to the Capspin official website. It has its own style and learning path. Learn how to spin a marker cap using both hands and a smooth twirl.

1. Setup

  • Use a marker cap that is not cracked.
  • Sit at a table to keep practice controlled.
  • Warm up fingers and wrists for 1 minute.

2. Two-Hand Hold

  • Hold the cap lightly between thumb and index finger of your right hand.
  • Place your left hand close to catch and guide.
  • Keep elbows relaxed and shoulders low.

3. Twirl Motion

  • Push the cap with your right thumb so it rolls over your index finger.
  • As it turns, let your left hand guide the next touch.
  • Transfer back and forth: right hand start, left hand control, right hand catch.

4. Build Speed

  • Begin slowly for clean control.
  • Do 10 good reps, then increase speed a little.
  • Focus on smooth circles, not force.

5. Air Twirl Style

  • Use your right hand to pop the cap up 3 to 6 inches.
  • Twist your wrist while it is in the air to add spin.
  • Catch with your left hand first, then return to right hand.

6. One-Hand Style

  • Keep the cap on your index finger and drive with your thumb.
  • Let it loop around your fingers without using the other hand.
  • Start with short spins, then connect 5 loops in a row.
