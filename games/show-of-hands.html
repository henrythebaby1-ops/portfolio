HOW IT WORKS
01

Grant camera access

Detection runs locally in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

02

Get in frame

Keep your hand(s) visible and reasonably lit. The viewfinder brackets show what's tracked.

03

Gesture on cue

Rock = closed fist. Paper = open palm. Scissors = index + middle finger only.

LIVE

TIPS

  • A held fist pulls steadily — pumping (fist → open → fist) pulls harder.
  • Stay roughly on your side of frame.
